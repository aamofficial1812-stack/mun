About the Conference

A diplomatic floor for the arguments that matter — built by students, for students who take the world seriously.

AIKTC Model United Nations is presented by the Debate & Dialogue Club of AIKTC, bringing together delegates to practice the discipline of diplomacy: research, negotiation, and the patient construction of consensus.

Across two committees — the United Nations Human Rights Council and the All India Political Parties Meet — delegates will represent positions that are not their own, argue them with rigor, and search for resolutions that could plausibly hold in the real world.

This is not a debate for its own sake. It is training in the hardest kind of listening: the kind that has to end in a text everyone can sign.

Committee Overview

The Human Rights Council is the United Nations' principal forum for the protection and promotion of human rights worldwide. In this committee, delegates represent member and observer states, examining the issues on the Council's agenda through formal debate, negotiation, and the drafting of resolutions.

Committee Format

Delegates will move through opening statements, formal and informal negotiation, and resolution drafting — working toward outcomes that stay faithful to their assigned state's genuine foreign policy positions. The committee agenda will be released closer to the conference.

All India Political Parties Meet

Committee Overview

AIPPM simulates a multi-party political consultation, placing delegates inside India's own federal legislative debate. Rather than representing sovereign states, delegates represent the leadership of Indian political parties — balancing ideology, coalition arithmetic, and the practical realities of governance.

Committee Format

Delegates will negotiate as party representatives through formal statements, coalition-building, and resolution drafting — in pursuit of consensus that a real multi-party meet could plausibly reach, without abandoning their party's core positions. The committee agenda will be announced closer to the conference.
