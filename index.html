About the Conference

A diplomatic floor for the arguments that matter — built by students, for students who take the world seriously.

AIKTCMUN is presented by the Debate & Dialogue Club of AIKTC, bringing together delegates to practice the discipline of diplomacy: research, negotiation, and the patient construction of consensus.

The conference runs across two committees — UNHRC, the United Nations Human Rights Council, and AIPPM, the All India Political Parties Meet. Each committee is its own room, with its own procedure and its own stakes.

This is not a debate for its own sake. It is training in the hardest kind of listening: the kind that has to end in a text everyone can sign.
