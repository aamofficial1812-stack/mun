Committee Overview

The Human Rights Council is the United Nations' principal forum for the protection and promotion of human rights worldwide. In this committee, delegates represent member and observer states, examining the issues on the Council's agenda through formal debate, negotiation, and the drafting of resolutions.

Committee Format

Delegates will move through opening statements, formal and informal negotiation, and resolution drafting — working toward outcomes that stay faithful to their assigned state's genuine foreign policy positions. The committee agenda will be released closer to the conference.
